Investment Mayhem

A multiplayer market simulation. 624 real listed companies, 10 index funds, 260 properties, crypto, commodities and angel deals — all on one shared clock.

Ticker symbols and company names are real; every price in this game is simulated and has no relationship to the actual market. Not financial advice.

Paste the firebaseConfig object from your Firebase console. It is stored in this browser only.

Anyone who opens that link joins your world without needing a Firebase project of their own.
